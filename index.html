Confirm a human.
A cloned voice can authorize a payment in minutes. These five steps stop it.
  1. Pause. Never act on the first contact.
  2. Call back on a number you already have.
  3. Ask for the verification phrase.
  4. Second approver above the threshold.
  5. Report it the same day.
